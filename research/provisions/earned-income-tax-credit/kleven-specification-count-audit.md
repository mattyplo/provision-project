# Kleven (2024) specification count — primary-text discrepancy audit

**Reviewed 2026-11-22.** **Primary texts:** [Kleven, *Journal of Public Economics* 236 (2024), 105135, journal-formatted full text hosted by Berkeley](https://eml.berkeley.edu/~saez/course/klevenJpubE24EITC.pdf), **§5.4, printed p. 16**, and [May 2024 author revision](https://www.henrikkleven.com/uploads/3/7/3/1/37310663/kleven_eitc_may2024.pdf), **§5.4, printed p. 24**. Both texts were accessible as searchable indexed source extracts. The journal-formatted text also gives **304** non-1993 estimates in Figure 9.

## What the source actually says
The author describes **four samples × four outcomes × four control sets** across **seven reform experiments** (five federal reforms and two stacked specifications), then explicitly states **432 specifications** and says the online appendix presents event studies for all 432. Both the journal-formatted and May 2024 author text give this count.

## Arithmetic discrepancy — unresolved
- Literal full Cartesian product: **4 × 4 × 4 × 7 = 448**, **not 432**.
- Published §5.4 Figure 9 reports **304** estimates **excluding the 1993 reform**; 432 − 304 = **128**, consistent with two 64-specification 1993-related experiment blocks, but **does not by itself explain** the 16 absent cells relative to a full 448-cell Cartesian product.
- The paper does **not explain the 16-cell difference in the inspected §5.4 text**. It may reflect omitted/nonestimable combinations or another design restriction, but **do not assert an explanation without the appendix or replication artifacts**.
- The **432 count is confirmed as author-reported in the published full text**, but **the exact grid decomposition is not reconciled**. This is **a source-level inconsistency, not merely a project multiplication mistake**.

## Public-facing disposition
Use: **“Kleven (2024) reports 432 event-study specifications; the paper describes seven reform experiments and four choices each of sample, outcome and controls, which would imply 448 combinations if fully crossed. The reason for the difference is unresolved in our source inspection.”** If too much detail for the main narrative, say **“432 author-reported specifications (grid count not independently reconciled)”** and link this audit.

**Evidence classification:** author-reported count **verified**; independent enumeration **not verified**. Figure 8 averages −0.32pp outside 1993 and +4.02pp for 1993 remain **researcher-reported**, not our replicated estimates. Do not silently alter 432 to 448 or accuse the author of a proven computational error. An online-appendix figure-by-figure enumeration would be needed to resolve the gap.
