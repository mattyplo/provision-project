## IRS SOI — direct table check
[IRS “Earned Income Tax Credit statistics”](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/individuals/earned-income-tax-credit/earned-income-tax-credit-statistics), accessed 2026-11-22, heading **“Earned Income Tax statistics from 1999 forward”**:
- **TY2023\*** row: **23.2 million returns**, **$63.8 billion total EITC**. Draft's “approximately 23 million / $64 billion” is acceptable rounding but precise row is now available.
- **TY2024\*** row: **24.1 million returns**, **$70.6 billion total EITC**. **This is a different product from IRS's TY2024 state processing table (~24m / $69.6b processed 2025).** Never silently replace or merge these figures.
- **Crucial source footnote:** IRS says the starred **2021–2024 figures are actual volume, not estimates**, obtained from an EITC fact sheet sourced to the **Compliance Data Warehouse (CDW)**. The page's generic lead says statistics are sample estimates, but the starred-row footnote **specifically overrides that for these years**. The earlier project's blanket characterization of TY2023 as a sample-based SOI estimate is too broad; cite the footnote precisely.
- Page says figures count returns credited after math error and exclude later changes due to amended returns, examinations and other adjustments.
