## Important new complication: Schedule 3-A / proposed PRWORA rule
The [IRS eligibility page](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/individuals/earned-income-tax-credit/who-qualifies-for-the-earned-income-tax-credit-eitc) currently states **“Beginning with tax year 2026, you may have to complete Schedule 3-A, Federal Public Benefit”** when claiming EITC. The IRS also hosts a [**draft** 2026 Schedule 3-A, marked DO NOT FILE](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-dft/f1040s3a--dft.pdf). **Neither an IRS web-page statement nor a draft form by itself establishes that the proposed regulatory eligibility restriction was finalized by Oct. 31.**

[91 FR 53812 (Aug. 20, 2026)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-08-20/pdf/FR-2026-08-20.pdf) is explicitly a **notice of proposed rulemaking** (REG-119882-25). [91 FR 64631 (Oct. 9)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-10-09/html/2026-20790.htm) **cancels only the hearing**, not the proposed rule. [CRS Oct. 26, 2026](https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/2026-10-26_IF13337_e680915aaae1ae3f49b05f7cb99b1203e6f763c3.pdf) still calls it a proposal. A targeted search for REG-119882-25 / RIN 1545-BS06 did **not identify a final rule** through cutoff. **Disposition: “proposed as of Oct. 26; no final rule located by targeted search as of Oct. 31,” not unconditional proof of no final rule.** A formal Federal Register API/docket search is still needed for exhaustive certification. The form/guidance discrepancy should be escalated, not resolved by assumption.
