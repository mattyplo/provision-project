## Priority outstanding issues
**P0 — TY2026 special eligibility:** [eligibility-exceptions-audit.md](eligibility-exceptions-audit.md) draws on **2025** Publication 596. The draft properly flags this but a fully certified TY2026 eligibility matrix still requires a §32 and TY2026 instructions crosswalk, especially SSN/self-only rules, separated spouses, Form 8862 bans and combat-pay/clergy treatment. If instructions are not final, disclose the precise status.

**P0 — claim-level source QA:** Most citations point to documents, not exact sections/tables/pages. A human editor should click each high-salience link, verify availability, and add stable pinpoint citations for fiscal figures, participation denominators, and empirical tables. No claim that every hyperlink was retested during this review.

**P1 — study comparability:** Eissa/Liebman working vs published, HMS working vs published, and Kleven author revision vs journal should be consistently marked as distinct *versions*, not independent studies. The draft currently handles this; retain through any later rewrite.

**P1 — source publication and access status:** IRS webpages are mutable; record retrieval dates and, where possible, stable IRS revenue procedures or archived snapshots. Proposed PRWORA rule must stay labeled **proposed as of 2026-10-31**; do not extrapolate later status from draft Schedule 3-A.

**P1 — 1978 and other chronology nuances:** The record is expressly a selected milestone timeline. Before presenting it as an exhaustive amendment history, review additional laws and direct/indirect interactions; **not a blocker** for a clearly labeled selected history.

**P2 — empirical reproducibility:** No microdata rerun and not all appendices examined. This is a limitation to disclose, **not** a prerequisite to publishing an accurately attributed evidence synthesis.

## Editorial recommendation
The draft answers all seven core questions, is readable, and generally keeps statutory facts, official estimates, empirical findings and project interpretation distinct. **Do not mark the provision fully publication-ready yet.** Close P0 eligibility and citation pinpoints, then perform a final link/claim review. Keep research disagreements and 'Unknown/insufficient evidence' in the public-facing record rather than relegating them solely to appendices.

**Provenance:** This review reads the actual draft and the project research-quality and legislative-history files, and independently spot-checks the 1978 enacted-law citation and the Schanzenbach–Strain bibliographic record. It does **not** independently revalidate every figure in the draft.
