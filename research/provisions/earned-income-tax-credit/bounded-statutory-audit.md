**Research cutoff:** 2026-10-31. **Audit date:** 2026-11-16. Scope is the §32 EITC legal record, not all indirect economic effects of other statutes. **Status:** October direct-§32 check closed on OLRC and GPO cross-check; exhaustive *full-text inspection of every October law* not claimed.

## 1984: expanded credit and renumbering (OLRC statutory notes)
[OLRC historical §32 notes](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-124/t26/s32/) and [1994 Code historical version](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=1994&req=granuleid%3AUSC-1994-title26-chapter1-subchapterA-part4-subpartF):
- P.L. **98-369 §471(c)** renumbered EITC **§43 to §32** (a **codification/renumbering**, not creation of the original 1975 credit).
- P.L. **98-369 §1042(a),(b),(d)(1)** raised the rate from **10% to 11%**, increased maximum credit from **$500 to $550**, and changed the phaseout rate/income parameters.
- **§1042(e)** provides application to taxable years beginning **after Dec. 31, 1984** (generally TY1985). Separately **§423(c)(3)** has its own §423(d) effective date, also after Dec. 31, 1984. Do not assume every 1984 technical provision has identical application.
