# EITC legislative history — direct committee-report verification and chronology

**Snapshot:** 2026-10-31. **Audit date:** 2026-11-16. This is an evidence ledger, not a comprehensive amendment-by-amendment enacted-law certification.

## Original Senate Finance Committee report — independently inspected
**Primary source:** [S. Rep. 94-36 (Mar. 17, 1975), *Tax Reduction Act of 1975*](https://www.finance.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/tax1.pdf), printed pp. **10–11** (reasons), **32–35** (general explanation), **23** (estimated revenue effect), and **81** (Sen. Brock supplemental views). The report discusses **the Senate committee bill**, not a substitute for the final enacted P.L. 94-12.

- **Committee's stated objectives:** offset some payroll/self-employment tax burden on low-income workers; make work more attractive; encourage employment and reduce welfare rolls. The committee explicitly stated that the credit **did not reduce Social Security taxes themselves**. (pp. 10–11, 32–33.) These are **contemporaneous legislative rationales**, not empirically established outcomes.
- **Design rationale:** Senate committee preferred 10% credit up to $400 for taxpayers maintaining a household with dependent children, rather than House bill's broader 5%/$200 credit; committee discussed avoiding benefits for some students/retirees and targeting families with children. (pp. 32–34.)
- **Contemporaneous fiscal estimate:** committee projected **$1.5 billion reduction in 1975 income tax liabilities** and **$0.1 billion reduced AFDC payments**, based on the committee bill (p. 34). Not an ex-post estimate, and not comparable directly to current JCT figures.
- **Contemporaneous criticism:** Sen. Bill Brock's supplemental views (p. 81) argued the measure would have limited immediate stimulus or welfare reform and risked complicating interactions with AFDC and other programs. This is **attributed dissent**, not a factual project conclusion.
- **Enacted outcome:** [P.L. 94-12 §204 and §209(b)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/STATUTE-89/pdf/STATUTE-89-Pg26.pdf), original §43, enacted March 29, 1975; 10%/$400 formula, initially temporary. **Do not conflate committee section 203 with enacted §204.**
