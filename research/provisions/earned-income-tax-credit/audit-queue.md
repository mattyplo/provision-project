## Audit protocol
1. Preserve each archived model output verbatim; corrections live in derivative files.
2. For each claim, record **source ID, exact document version/date, URL, page/section/table, inspected status, disposition, and limitations**.
3. A model saying 'directly inspected' is **not** independent inspection. A retrieved web snippet is not equivalent to full-text scrutiny of an empirical paper.
4. Treat official fiscal projections, observed administrative data, statutory law, attributed policy rationale, and project inference as separate evidence types.
5. Unknown economic incidence is acceptable. Neither claim counts nor refundable dollars alone prove who ultimately benefits.
